I am a Ph.D. student at The University of Hong Kong (HKU), advised by [Prof. Hongyang Du](https://hongyangdu.github.io/). I joined the [Network Intelligence and Computing Ecosystem (NICE) Lab](https://hongyangdu.github.io/nice/) in June 2025. Previously, I completed my master's studies at the [Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS)](https://english.ict.cas.cn/), advised by [Prof. Zhenyu Li](https://www.ict.cas.cn/sourcedb/cn/jssrck/201111/t20111114_3395505.html). I completed my undergraduate studies at Hunan University.

My research lies at the intersection of large language models and networked systems. I focus on building reliable and efficient LLM agents that can adapt to changing network and execution conditions. My recent work explores network-aware tool routing, reliable agent orchestration, and selective on-policy distillation for LLM post-training. More broadly, I am interested in combining learning algorithms with system design to improve the practical performance of intelligent systems.
